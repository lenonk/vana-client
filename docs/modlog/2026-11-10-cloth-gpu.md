## Cloth on the GPU (2026-11-10)

Issue lenonk/vana-client#155 (its rendering item). MODLOG "Cloth" has the retail facts; this changes where ours runs.

- render/cloth.rs: each model's cloth sheets (the ones SkinMesh numbers, the same MAX_CLOTH rule) go up once:
  per sheet a record (counts, pins, damping, gravity, inertia, k1 / k2, its first particle), every particle's skin
  influences, the edges, the triangles and its spheres resolved from the skeleton (an index past the table is no
  sphere, an unused slot takes no edges, as before).
- Each actor keeps its particles (cur, prev, normal), its spheres' last centres and two scratch tables in a shared
  state buffer, a separate range (a clone of the model's ActorGpu starts without one); the buffer grows by a GPU
  copy and a freed range is reused.
- A compute pass after the bones are posed: one invocation a sheet runs client/cloth.rs's loops in the same serial
  order (FUN_10023290's reset snap or skin and carry, FUN_10023700's Verlet and four constraint passes,
  FUN_10023bd0's owners and sweeps, FUN_10024900's normals), reading this frame's bones from the pose region; off
  screen FUN_100231f0's carry. A second dispatch writes the particles into the pose region in model space (with the
  ride since the last step), where vs_skinned already read them. Nothing goes up per actor per frame but the job.
- The step now skins the pinned particles with the bones as drawn (the GPU pose, head look included); the CPU step
  posed them again without the head look. The CPU posing for cloth, its simulation and the per-frame particle
  upload are gone. The client keeps only the 30 Hz tick and where the model stood (cloth::Track).
- client/cloth.rs's Cloth stays as the reference (and cloth-shot's). Tests on model 1367 (King Destin's cape, 8
  spheres, standing in the wind then walking): each GPU step from the CPU's state matches it to f32 rounding (about
  2e-6) on every step but two; on those two a CPU nudged by 1e-6 departs from itself by as much (a particle the last
  sweep left on a sphere's surface sits on the next inside / outside test). Run separately the two stay within
  0.035 units.
- Ours: the sweep loop stops after 4096 sub-steps (one a radius; no sane sphere reaches it).
- Seen live (Lnxcsfix, `!costume 67`): the cape hangs, swings out walking and settles.
